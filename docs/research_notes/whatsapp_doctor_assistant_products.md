# AI front-desk / patient-communication agents that answer patient WhatsApp (and phone/SMS) messages for doctors and clinics — competitor landscape as of 20 Oct 2026

Method note: WebSearch/WebFetch hit a session limit part-way through; most vendor pages, press releases and market reports were fetched directly with curl on 20 Oct 2026 and read in full. Several vendor sites (hyro.ai, voiceoc.com/healthcare, ekacare.com, practo.com/plus/ai, doctolib.fr, tatkaldoctor.com/whatsapp-api-pricing) are JavaScript-rendered or bot-blocked and returned no text; for those I relied on press releases, help-centre articles and third-party reviews, flagged below. All prices are list prices as displayed on the cited page on the date noted; most India prices are ex-GST (18%).

## Key Question 1: Which India-focused products answer patient WhatsApp messages on a doctor's number (reply, triage, book, remind, escalate)?

### Takeaway
No major Indian EMR/marketplace incumbent (Practo, HealthPlix, Eka Care) publicly ships an LLM agent that auto-replies to inbound patient WhatsApp; their WhatsApp use is outbound reminders/Rx delivery. The "AI receptionist for Indian clinics" space is instead populated by 2025-2026 micro-startups (ConnectAI/Dhanwantri, Tatkal Doctor, Pluxo, HuskyVoice, Patient Square, Hayati Nexus, MantraAssist, Voiceoc) priced at roughly ₹500–₹5,000 per clinic or clinician per month, plus generic WhatsApp BSPs (AiSensy, Wati, Interakt) selling "AI agent" add-ons for ₹1,350–₹3,000/month that clinics can self-configure.

### Inferences
- The Indian doctor-side WhatsApp-agent market is fragmented and very early: most named vendors are 2024–2026 launches with no disclosed funding, and traction claims (400+ clinics, 500+ partners) are self-reported.
- Price anchoring in India is ₹1,500–₹5,000/month per clinic or clinician, i.e., well under one receptionist salary (₹18k–₹25k); this leaves little room for per-conversation LLM costs unless volumes are low.
- Incumbent EMRs (Eka, HealthPlix, Practo) have the distribution (14k–hundreds of thousands of doctors) and could bundle an inbound agent quickly; none has, as of Oct 2026, which is both a gap and a threat.

### Gaps
- No primary-source pricing for Tatkal Doctor's WhatsApp plan (JS page), Voiceoc, MantraAssist, Hayati Nexus, Pluxo.
- MantraAssist's "$15M Series A from Impanix Capital" and "500+ partners" could not be corroborated.
- No evidence found for Kenko, MediBuddy, Doctor's Prime, QuickBlox, Kapture, Zence, Hippo/HelloDoctor, Carenet, Gupshup healthcare (gupshup.io/industries/healthcare 404s), Yellow.ai healthcare (page is generic) offering a doctor-number WhatsApp reply agent; treat as not present in this niche.

## Key Question 2: Which global products do this (AI front door across phone/SMS/chat/WhatsApp)?

### Takeaway
Globally the category is dominated by US voice-first "AI patient access" platforms (Assort Health, Hyro, Hello Patient, Parakeet, Talkie.ai, Simbie, Zocdoc Zo, Luma Navigator, Artera, Notable, Klara/ModMed) that add SMS/web chat, not WhatsApp; WhatsApp-native doctor-side products exist in WhatsApp-dominant markets — Leona (Mexico, inbox + AI triage/summaries on the doctor's own WhatsApp), Docplanner's Noa Booking (voice with WhatsApp confirmations, 13 countries), Vocca (France/Europe phone agent, 15,000 practitioners), and Doctolib's Assistant téléphonique (€99/month).

## Key Question 4: Architecture — LLM vs rules, safety/escalation, WhatsApp connectivity (Meta Cloud API vs BSP), EHR integration, Indian-language support

### Takeaway
The best-documented architecture (Hyro) is a hybrid: a deterministic knowledge/capabilities graph that calls multiple specialised LLMs/SLMs at decision points, with five models orchestrating appointment scheduling after an earlier pure-LLM flow hallucinated non-existent slots; every mature vendor advertises contextual human handoff. Indian WhatsApp products connect through the official Meta Cloud API or a BSP's WhatsApp Business API, support Hindi/Hinglish and 6–11 regional languages, and escalate clinical questions to the doctor; Voiceoc discloses GPT-4; Assort uses a proprietary model "Synapse"; Docplanner uses Twilio ConversationRelay with swappable STT/TTS.

### Cited Findings

## Key Question 6: Market size and growth for conversational AI in healthcare / patient engagement

### Takeaway

### Inferences
- The "patient engagement & support" slice (~22–30% of a ~$17–21B 2025–26 market) implies a ~$4–6B global 2026 addressable pool for front-door agents, of which India is a small but fastest-growing share.
- Sizing an India WhatsApp doctor-agent TAM bottom-up (e.g., HealthPlix's 14k doctors, Practo's provider base, ~1.3M registered allopathic doctors) at ₹2,000–₹5,000/month would be more defensible than top-down analyst numbers, which differ by >8x across publishers.

### Gaps
- No report sizes "AI receptionist / WhatsApp patient-communication agents for doctors in India" specifically; no count of Indian doctors using WhatsApp Business API was found.
- Gartner, Rock Health and CB Insights figures for this niche were not retrieved due to tool limits.
