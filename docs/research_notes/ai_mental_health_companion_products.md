## Key Question 1: Global leaders — product-by-product landscape (company, product, approach, evidence, pricing, model, funding, design)

### Takeaway
By Oct 2026 the category has split into (a) regulated/enterprise-first hybrids (Wysa, Limbic, Woebot-as-B2B) that sell to NHS/employers/health plans, (b) VC-funded LLM-native consumer "AI therapists" (Slingshot's Ash, Sonia, Abby, Elomia) that launched free and are now monetising at ~$20/month, and (c) a graveyard of B2C and voice-biomarker players (Woebot consumer app June 2025, Youper Sept 2026, Kintsugi Feb 2026, Character.ai Chapter 11 Oct 2026) — while general-purpose LLMs (ChatGPT) added distress routing, parental controls and clinician-graded safety evals in Aug–Oct 2025.

**Dartmouth "Therabot" (research prototype, not a product)**
- First RCT of a generative-AI therapy chatbot, NEJM AI, 27 Mar 2025 (Heinz et al.); 210 adults randomised (106 Therabot, 104 waitlist) with MDD, GAD or high risk for eating disorders; 4 weeks unlimited access then 4 weeks unprompted; depression symptoms −51%, GAD −31%, body-image/weight concerns −19% (changes from baseline, eating-disorder arm significantly outpaced control); users engaged ~6 hours on average (≈8 therapy sessions); therapeutic alliance "in line with" in-person providers; ~75% of Therabot users not in other treatment — [Dartmouth News, 27 Mar 2025](https://home.dartmouth.edu/news/2025/03/first-therapy-chatbot-trial-yields-mental-health-benefits); paper DOI [10.1056/AIoa2400802](https://ai.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/AIoa2400802)
- Design: open-ended generative dialog trained on "an original training set the researchers developed from current, evidence-based best practices for psychotherapy and CBT"; in development since 2019 with continuous psychologist/psychiatrist consultation; high-risk content (e.g., suicidal ideation) triggers an on-screen prompt/button for 911 or crisis line; researchers reviewed conversations for best-practice adherence; the study team was "equipped to intervene—possibly right away"; earlier evals >2 years before showed >90% of responses consistent with best practice — [Dartmouth News](https://home.dartmouth.edu/news/2025/03/first-therapy-chatbot-trial-yields-mental-health-benefits)
- Authors' caution: "no generative AI agent is ready to operate fully autonomously in mental health" (Heinz) — [Dartmouth News](https://home.dartmouth.edu/news/2025/03/first-therapy-chatbot-trial-yields-mental-health-benefits)

## Key Question 3: Neurodivergence screening (ADHD/autism/dyslexia) — what is cleared vs wellness-only

### Takeaway

## Key Question 4: Architecture facts (rules vs LLM, safety classifiers, crisis routing, evals, red-teaming)

### Takeaway
Public designs cluster into three patterns: (1) rules-first with LLM in constrained roles plus human oversight (Wysa, Woebot-legacy); (2) a proprietary "clinical layer" wrapped around an LLM with ML triage and escalation (Limbic Layer, Headspace Ebb's "proprietary safety system", Elomia); (3) a purpose-built foundation model trained on behavioral-health data with RL and a crisis gateway (Slingshot Ash, Dartmouth Therabot) — while OpenAI's 2025 work shows the industry-standard eval stack: taxonomies, adversarial offline evals, clinician-graded scoring, classifiers and per-message routing.

### Cited Findings
- Wysa: hybrid "rule based algorithms and large language modelling"; institutional versions range from "primarily rule-based" to "rule-based + LLM prompts"; "All conversational responses… are AI powered"; PII redaction within 24h; encryption; "human in the loop" across care pathways — [Wysa FAQ](https://www.wysa.com/faq); [wysa.com](https://www.wysa.com/)
- Woebot (legacy): scripted multi-choice responses pre-written by clinicians so "the AI could not say something dangerous or unvetted"; users reported false safety flags — [ai-health-apps.com](https://ai-health-apps.com/reviews/woebot-review/); [App Store review](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1305375832)
- Limbic: "Limbic Layer… sits between users and a large language model"; Triage Agent ML "predicts diagnoses"; Therapy Agent "delivers CBT through generative chat while managing clear escalation pathways to clinical staff"; Class IIa with ISO 14971 risk management — [limbic.ai](https://www.limbic.ai/); [MedAI Verdict](https://medaiverdict.com/tools/limbic)
- Slingshot Ash: foundation model for psychology; pre-train on behavioral-health data → clinical fine-tune (when to challenge/end conversations) → RL from conversation signals; "guardrails and gateway-to-human-support design"; "designed to end conversations instead of maximizing engagement"; opt-in use of user conversations for training/grounding — [HLTH](https://hlth.com/insights/news/slingshot-ai-launches-ash-first-ai-designed-specifically-for-therapy-with-93m-funding-2025-07-25); [talktoash.com/news](https://www.talktoash.com/news); [EULA](https://www.talktoash.com/research)
- Therabot: generative model trained on an original, expert-written dataset of evidence-based psychotherapy/CBT; risk detection triggers crisis-button prompt; conversations reviewed by researchers; study team on standby to intervene; >90% best-practice consistency in pre-trial evals — [Dartmouth](https://home.dartmouth.edu/news/2025/03/first-therapy-chatbot-trial-yields-mental-health-benefits)
- Headspace Ebb: voice/text, memory across sessions, "Conversations are evaluated to make sure Ebb is always helpful", "Clinical experts have rigorously tested Ebb for safety… proprietary safety system to guide members to additional care or crisis support" — [headspace.com/ebb](https://www.headspace.com/ebb)
- OpenAI: per-message router to GPT-5-thinking for sensitive conversations; classifier-blocked outputs; stronger minor protections; break nudges; 988/Samaritans referral; human review for harm-to-others; taxonomies + adversarial offline evals graded by clinicians; metrics for psychosis/mania, self-harm, emotional reliance; acknowledged long-context safety degradation — [OpenAI Aug 2025](https://openai.com/index/helping-people-when-they-need-it-most/); [OpenAI Oct 2025](https://openai.com/index/strengthening-chatgpt-responses-in-sensitive-conversations/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/29/openai-rolls-out-safety-routing-system-parental-controls-on-chatgpt/)
- Kintsugi: voice-biomarker "dam" audio-classification model and dataset open-sourced (Hugging Face) after shutdown — [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/kintsugihealth)
- Elomia: "validated LLM-based system"; "Safe AI detects when a person needs something more than a chatbot and redirects them… to a therapist or hotlines" — [elomia.com](https://elomia.com/)
- Koko: AI + adaptive keyword engine to detect high-risk content on partner platforms — [kokocares.org](https://www.kokocares.org/)
- MindPeers: cross-signal risk model (therapy data, assessments, wearables, biomarkers) for cohort-level early alerts — [mindpeers.co](https://www.mindpeers.co/)
- Abby: "Watch Abby think… a process designed with licensed therapists. She maps how much of your story she knows" (visible reasoning step before replies) — [abby.gg](https://abby.gg/)

### Inferences
- A defensible design for a new entrant is Wysa/Limbic-style: deterministic CBT/safety "spine" (scripted protocols, validated questionnaires, crisis state machine with hotline hand-off) with the LLM confined to reflective dialogue and content selection, plus OpenAI-style taxonomy-driven offline evals graded by clinicians and logged per-message risk classification.
- Slingshot's and Therabot's results suggest purpose-trained models reduce sycophancy and increase alliance, but both still rely on human monitoring/escalation rather than autonomous crisis handling.

### Gaps
- No vendor publishes model names, parameter counts, RAG use, or red-team methodology; Slingshot's "Inside how Slingshot trains Ash's foundation model" (13 Nov 2025) and Wysa's engineering posts could not be fetched. Limbic's "Our commitment to safety" (18 Jul 2024) not fetched.

## Key Question 6: Business-model lessons (B2C vs B2B2C, reimbursement, regulation, 2025–26 trends)

### Takeaway
Pure B2C AI mental-health subscriptions have repeatedly failed or retreated (Woebot Jun 2025, Youper Sep 2026, Kintsugi Feb 2026, Character.ai Oct 2026), survivors monetise via employers/insurers/NHS/health systems (Wysa "11M lives covered", Limbic, Woebot-B2B, MindPeers, YourDOST, Lissun), and the 2025–26 frontier is (a) VC-backed foundation-model therapy apps now flipping on $20/mo paywalls, (b) US Medicare DMHT codes G0552–G0554 for FDA-authorized devices, and (c) state laws (Illinois PA 104-0054) restricting AI "therapy" without licensed oversight.

### Inferences
- The viable path for a new AI counsellor is B2B2C-first (employers/campuses/insurers/government helplines) with a freemium consumer funnel; in India, Tele-MANAS integration or state health-department contracts (as J&K's WhatsApp bot shows) are the public-sector analogue of NHS deals.
- Clinical claims (detecting conditions, suicide prevention) trigger SaMD regulation (FDA Class II, UKCA IIa, CDSCO) and now state licensing laws; competitors are deliberately staying "wellness" on the consumer side and reserving clinical claims for clinician-supervised B2B deployments.
