# Healthcare Agents and Assistants

Two healthcare AI products, India-first.

| Folder | Product | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `doctor-whatsapp-agent/` | **SevaDesk** – AI front desk that answers patient WhatsApp messages on a doctor's number, triages emergencies, and drafts clinical replies for the doctor to approve | MVP in progress |
| `ai-counsellor/` | **Saathi** – AI mental-wellbeing companion with layered crisis safety | Design + prompts only (build after SevaDesk) |
| `docs/` | Market research report (MD + PDF), build plans, system prompts, and the raw research notes with sources | Done (20 Oct 2026) |
